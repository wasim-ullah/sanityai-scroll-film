FAQs

Questions we hear before every first call.

What does SanityAI actually do?

We bring together four things most companies buy from different suppliers: marketing strategy, search (including AI assistants), automation and paid ads. One team looks after the whole journey from first click to closed sale, so nothing falls through the gaps.

Who do you work with?

Growing businesses in the Gulf and beyond that already spend on marketing and want more back from it. We've worked in insurance, real estate, hospitality, healthcare, business services and online retail.

What is AI search, and do I need to think about it?

More and more people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini or Claude for recommendations before they visit any website. If those answers mention your competitors and not you, you're missing customers you never knew about. We help make sure you're part of the answer.

How soon will we see results?

Tracking fixes and automations usually make a difference in the first month. Ads tend to improve within 4 to 8 weeks. Search takes longer, usually 3 to 6 months, but keeps building over time.

Which tools do you use?

Mostly the ones you already have. For ads we work with Google, Meta, LinkedIn and Bing. For tracking, Google Analytics and Tag Manager. For automation, tools like n8n, HubSpot and GoHighLevel, connected to the sales system you use today.

How does pricing work?

We start with a fixed-price review and 90-day plan. After that, most clients work with us month to month, sized to what they need. You see the plan before you commit to anything ongoing.

Will we own everything you build?

Yes. Ad accounts, tracking, automations, content and data all stay in your name and remain yours if we stop working together.
