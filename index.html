FAQs

Questions we hear before every first call.

What does SanityAI actually do?

We connect four things most companies buy separately: marketing strategy, search optimisation (including AI search), automation, and paid media. One team owns the whole path from first impression to closed sale, so nothing gets lost between agencies.

Who do you work with?

Growing companies in the GCC and beyond that already spend on marketing and want it to return more. Our work spans insurance, real estate, hospitality, healthcare, B2B services and e-commerce. If you sell something people search for, we can help.

What is AI search optimisation, and do I need it?

It is making sure assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude know your brand and recommend it. A growing share of buyers ask an assistant before they visit any website. If those answers name your competitors and not you, that demand never reaches you.

How fast will we see results?

Tracking fixes and automations usually show impact within the first month. Paid media typically improves within 4 to 8 weeks as the platforms learn from cleaner data. Search and AI visibility compound over 3 to 6 months.

Do you work in Arabic?

Yes. We build voice agents, WhatsApp flows and campaigns in English and Arabic, and plan for both from the start rather than translating at the end.

Which tools and platforms do you use?

Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn and Bing for media; GA4, server-side Google Tag Manager and conversion APIs for tracking; n8n, HubSpot, GoHighLevel and your existing CRM for automation. We work with what you have before we suggest anything new.

How do engagements and pricing work?

We start with a fixed-fee audit and 90-day plan. After that, most clients keep us on a monthly retainer sized to the scope. You see the plan before you commit to anything ongoing.

Will we own the work?

Yes. Ad accounts, tracking, automations, content and data stay in your accounts and remain yours if we part ways.
